## Known confounds and what was not proven

The four constructed families are narrow; reference policies solve them perfectly without activating much failure pressure. No learned diagnostic or efficacy grid completed. Configured inference concurrency was one; observed model dispatches were zero. The experiment lacks a separate message-duplicate metric. First-progress timing excludes preparation, and consumers must read branch abort status alongside `complete_rollout`. Logical byte counters overlap and control units are not dollars, energy or physical I/O. GPU time and monetary cost were not measured. Finite process-race, late-receipt and fake-clock tests do not establish distributed or long-running reliability.
